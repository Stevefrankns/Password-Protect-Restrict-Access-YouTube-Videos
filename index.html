How to Password Protect and Restrict Access to YouTube Videos

Editorial Team · Updated May 2026 · 12 min read
Quick Answer

Can you password protect a YouTube video? No. YouTube does not offer a native feature to add a password field to a video. To restrict access, you must use YouTube's Private setting (which requires viewers to log in with an approved Google account) or the Unlisted setting (anyone with the link can view). For true file-level password protection and encryption, you must secure the video file locally using dedicated encryption software before uploading or sharing it via cloud drives.

Can You Password Protect a YouTube Video? — Full Guide

Millions of creators, educators, and businesses upload content daily, often asking: how to make a password protected YouTube video? The reality is that YouTube is fundamentally designed as a public broadcasting platform. It does not natively support an "open password" or "permissions password" that prompts a viewer to type in a secret code before playback begins.

Instead, YouTube relies on account-based permissions and hidden links. If you need to secure a video, you must choose between YouTube's built-in compromises, utilizing a competitor platform like Vimeo, or circumventing video hosting platforms entirely by encrypting the raw video file before sharing it.

YouTube Private vs Unlisted vs Public — Differences

Because you cannot directly add a password to a video on YouTube, understanding its privacy modes is essential for restricting access.

How to Share a YouTube Video Privately

The "Private" setting is the closest YouTube comes to secure access. When a video is marked private, it does not appear in search results, suggested videos, or on your channel. To share it, you must explicitly invite users via email.

Limitations of Private Videos:
  • Viewers must have a Google account to log in and verify their identity.
  • You are limited to sharing the video with a maximum of 50 users.
  • It is difficult to manage at scale (e.g., for a paid course or large corporate training).

Understanding YouTube Unlisted Videos

An "Unlisted" video operates like an unindexed webpage. It will not show up in YouTube search or subscriber feeds. However, the URL itself acts as the key. Anyone who has the link can view the video, regardless of whether they have a Google account.

While easier to share than a Private video, an Unlisted video offers zero real security. If someone posts the link on a public forum, the video effectively becomes public. You cannot restrict access or monitor who is watching an Unlisted video if the link is forwarded.

Alternatives to Password-Protected YouTube Videos

If you absolutely must stream the video online rather than sharing an encrypted file download, YouTube is not your only option. Competitor platforms offer the specific feature YouTube lacks.

Vimeo Password Protection

Unlike YouTube, Vimeo allows creators (usually on paid tiers) to set a strict password on a video. When a viewer clicks the link, they are met with a lock screen and a text field. They do not need a Vimeo account to watch; they only need to type in the password you provided.

While convenient, remember that streaming platforms still buffer the video to the viewer's device. For ultimate security, preventing document copying or unauthorized downloading still requires local file encryption.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you password protect a YouTube video?

No, YouTube does not have a native feature to add a password to a video. You can only restrict access using the "Private" setting (which requires specific Google account logins) or the "Unlisted" setting (which hides the video but allows anyone with the link to watch).

What is the alternative to password-protecting a YouTube video?

If you need a streaming link with a password, you can use Vimeo, which offers direct password protection on specific paid plans. If you need ultimate security, bypass streaming entirely: encrypt the video file locally using AES-256 software and share the locked file directly.

What is the difference between private, unlisted, and public on YouTube?

Public videos are visible to everyone and indexed in search. Unlisted videos are hidden from search and your channel but can be viewed by anyone who has the direct URL. Private videos are restricted entirely; only the channel owner and specifically invited Google accounts can view them.

Is an unlisted YouTube video completely private?

No. While it won't show up in search results, the URL is not secure. If an invited viewer copies the link and posts it publicly, anyone who clicks it can watch the video.
